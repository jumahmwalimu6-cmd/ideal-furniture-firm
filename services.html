Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I place an order?
Simply send us a WhatsApp message on +254 726 634 278, call us, or visit our showroom in Witeithie, Thika. We'll guide you through the entire process.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept M-Pesa, cash, and bank transfer. For large orders, we also offer a deposit-and-balance arrangement.
How long does custom furniture take?
Custom orders typically take 14–21 working days depending on the complexity. We'll give you an exact timeline when you place your order.
Is delivery free?
Delivery is free for all orders above KSh 20,000 within Witeithie and Thika Town. For other areas, a small delivery fee applies based on distance.
Do you offer a warranty?
Yes! All our furniture comes with a 1-year structural warranty. If anything goes wrong with the craftsmanship, we'll fix it free of charge.
Can I see furniture before buying?
Absolutely! Visit our showroom in Witeithie, Thika any day from Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. No appointment needed.
